# Sample documents

Drop public-domain or sample insurance-related PDFs here for local testing
(e.g. a state insurance department consumer guide, a sample policy summary,
or a synthetic underwriting/catastrophe-model document you have written
yourself). Do not commit confidential, proprietary, or copyrighted material
you do not have permission to distribute.

This folder is for local development convenience — the app itself accepts
PDFs via upload, so nothing here is required for the app to run.
